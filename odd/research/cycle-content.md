## 1962 — IT: Welcome to Derry, season 1

- Winter before the story: Matty Clements runs away; a family picks him up, turns back to Derry, and a winged creature attacks him. [sky-derry-e1]
- April 1962: Major Leroy Hanlon and Captain Pauly Russo arrive at the Air Force base (Colonel Fuller; General Shaw). The base prepares for possible nuclear war; a "Special Projects" bunker. [sky-derry-e1]
- Teddy, Phil, Lilly Bainbridge (former Juniper Hill patient) search for Matty; Ronnie Grogan (father Hank, projectionist) joins. At the Capitol, during The Music Man, Matty appears on screen; a creature kills Phil, Teddy and Susie; Lilly and Ronnie survive. [sky-derry-e1]
- Police Chief Clint Bowers pressures Lilly, threatening Juniper Hill; Hank is arrested for the killings. Leroy's wife Charlotte (activist) and son Will arrive. [sky-derry-e2]
- Shaw tests Leroy, who cannot feel fear after a war injury. Operation Precept: Shaw wants a "weapon" buried in Derry to win the Cold War; Airman Dick Hallorann uses his shine to find it. Hallorann reads Taniel (Rose's nephew) to locate the pillars; a raid at the Well House kills several, including Russo. [nerdist-operation-precept]
- The Shokopiwah built 13 pillars from shards of It's fallen star around the Western Woods: a cage that keeps It in Derry. [cbr-derry-retcons] It took Pennywise's form from Bob Gray, a real clown it met in 1908; his daughter Ingrid Kersh acts as It's familiar. [variety-derry-credits-scene]
- Episode 7: a mob led by the ousted Bowers chains the Black Spot (club for Black airmen) and sets it on fire; Pennywise appears in the flames. [collider-derry-e7] Ingrid tipped the mob to wake It. [cbr-derry-retcons] Hallorann gets Hank, Ronnie and Will out; Rich saves Marge and dies; Pennywise kills Stan Kersh. The army digs up a pillar and Shaw burns it at the base; Leroy learns the plan is to free It and use fear to control the country. [collider-derry-e7, nerdist-operation-precept]
- Finale: destroying the pillar wakes Pennywise early [cbr-derry-retcons]; freezing fog; Pennywise hypnotises children at a school assembly and leads them over the frozen Penobscot. Lilly, Ronnie and Marge must set a ceremonial dagger at a gnarled tree on the riverbank to lock It in; Rich's ghost helps; Hallorann distracts Pennywise; soldiers shoot Taniel; Pennywise kills Shaw. [ign-derry-finale, nerdist-operation-precept] Pennywise targets Marge because her son Richie Tozier will help kill It; It perceives past, present and future at once. [polygon-derry-ending]
- After: Leroy, shot in the leg, takes an honorable discharge and stays silent; Hallorann leaves for London to be a hotel chef [nerdist-operation-precept]; the Hanlons take over Rose's farm [polygon-derry-ending]; Hank and Ronnie flee to Canada [ign-derry-finale]. Post-credits: an elderly Ingrid at Juniper Hill meets young Beverly Marsh (no year stated). [variety-derry-credits-scene, ign-derry-finale]

Places 1962: Capitol cinema (massacre) [sky-derry-e1]; Derry Air Force Base (Shaw, Special Projects bunker, pillar burned) [sky-derry-e1, collider-derry-e7]; The Black Spot (burned by the mob) [collider-derry-e7]; Juniper Hill psychiatric hospital [sky-derry-e1, variety-derry-credits-scene]; Penobscot River and the gnarled tree on its bank (finale) [ign-derry-finale]; Western Woods (ring of 13 pillars) [cbr-derry-retcons]; the Well House (raid, Russo dies) [nerdist-operation-precept].

- June 1989: Henry starts carving his name into Ben on the Kissing Bridge; Ben escapes to the Barrens. Bev distracts the drugstore staff so the boys can take supplies.
