## Correction to an existing place (important)

- **library / 2016** currently says "Henry stabs Eddie, and Richie kills Henry" citing `collider-it-timeline`. That page places the stabbing of Eddie at **the inn** ("Back at the Inn…", Henry stabs Eddie through the face and escapes through a window), and in the library Henry **attacks Mike**, and Richie kills Henry with an axe. Proposed fix: "Henry attacks Mike here, and Richie arrives in time to kill Henry." [collider-it-timeline] Move Eddie's stabbing to the new 2016 inn entry.
- **well-house / 1962**: the raid where Pauly Russo dies is called "the Well House" by Nerdist [nerdist-operation-precept], but Sky places the same raid in the tunnels and sewers under **the Neibolt House** (episode 5 is titled "29 Neibolt Street"). [sky-derry-e5] Recommendation: treat the 1962 entry as 29 Neibolt Street and give it `spot.neibolt`, so Neibolt recurs in all three eras. Keep "Well House" only as Nerdist's wording.

## New places — 1962 (IT: Welcome to Derry, season 1)

- Sky calls it Matty's lookout in an abandoned building: his secret hangout with Teddy and Phil, where he once took Lilly. [sky-derry-e1] Collider calls the same hangout the standpipe; after Rich's death the girls go to clean it up and Marge finds one of his planes there. [collider-derry-e7]

- Rich dresses Marge's eye wound there and throws a glider from it; at the end Marge sees a glider of his that finally reached Main Street from the lookout. [sky-derry-e6, sky-derry-e8]
- Characters: `lilly`, `ronnie`, `will`, `rich`, `marge`. [sky-derry-e1, sky-derry-e4, sky-derry-e5, sky-derry-e6, collider-derry-e7]
