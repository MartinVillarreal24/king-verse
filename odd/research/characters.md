## The Losers' Club — 1988–89 (It, 2017) and 2016 (It Chapter Two, 2019)

Cast credits: kids from Variety's review credits [variety-it-review]; adults from TIME [time-it2-ending] and The Daily Beast [dailybeast-it2-review].

### Bill Denbrough
- Cycles: 1988–89, 2016. Actors: Jaeden Lieberher (kid) [variety-it-review]; James McAvoy (adult) [time-it2-ending, dailybeast-it2-review].
- Family: older brother of Georgie Denbrough. [variety-it-review, collider-it-timeline]
- Facts:
  - Makes a paper boat for Georgie, who loses it down a storm drain and is taken by Pennywise; Bill carries the guilt. [collider-it-timeline, variety-it-review]
  - In the cistern he fires Mike's bolt gun into It's head even though it is empty, proving belief matters. [collider-it-timeline]
  - As an adult he is a writer everyone teases for bad endings. [dailybeast-it2-review, collider-it-timeline]
  - Under Neibolt he finally forgives himself for Georgie's death. [collider-it-timeline] At the end he starts a new novel. [time-it2-ending]
- Object: **the paper boat**. Backing: the boat opens IT, and in 2016 Bill recovers it as his token for the Ritual of Chüd. [collider-it-timeline, time-it2-ending] Alternative: his bike Silver, which he buys back in a second-hand shop. [collider-it-timeline]
- Fear (verified): a zombie version of Georgie in his basement. [collider-it-timeline]

### Ben Hanscom
- Cycles: 1988–89, 2016. Actors: Jeremy Ray Taylor (kid) [variety-it-review]; Jay Ryan (adult) [time-it2-ending, dailybeast-it2-review].
- Facts:
  - Shy new kid who spends his time in the library. [variety-it-review] Beverly signs his yearbook, the only signature he gets. [collider-it-timeline]
  - Writes Beverly the "January embers" postcard poem without signing it. [collider-it-timeline]
  - In the cistern he wakes Beverly from the deadlights with a kiss. [collider-it-timeline, collider-it-ending]
  - As an adult he is an architect who lost his childhood weight. [dailybeast-it2-review]
  - In the final fight he saves Bev and tells her he loves her; at the end they leave together. [collider-it-timeline, time-it2-ending]
- Object: **the yearbook page Bev signed**, kept in his wallet and used as his token. [collider-it-timeline, time-it2-ending] Alternative: the postcard (but it is Bev's token; avoid duplicates).
- Fear (verified): headless victims of the Iron Works explosion chase him in the library. [collider-it-timeline]

### Beverly Marsh
- Cycles: 1988–89, 2016; also a brief cameo in the 1962 season's post-credits scene (no year stated there; see Do NOT). Actors: Sophia Lillis (kid; also the post-credits cameo) [variety-it-review, variety-derry-credits-scene]; Jessica Chastain (adult) [time-it2-ending, dailybeast-it2-review].
- Facts:
  - Confident tomboy trying to escape an abusive father. [variety-it-review, collider-it-timeline]
  - Drives a fire poker into Pennywise's head at Neibolt. [collider-it-timeline]

  - Learns Ben, not Bill, wrote the postcard; ends up with Ben. [collider-it-timeline, time-it2-ending]
- Family/links: an older Ingrid Kersh (1962 season) meets young Beverly at Juniper Hill; in 2016 "Mrs. Kersh" at her old home turns out to be Pennywise. [polygon-derry-ending, collider-it-timeline]
- Object: **the "January embers" postcard** — her token for the ritual. [collider-it-timeline, time-it2-ending] Alternative: the fire poker (verified, violent but not gory). [collider-it-timeline]
- Fear (verified): hair and blood bursting from her bathroom sink. [collider-it-timeline]
